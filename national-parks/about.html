About this site

Why I built it, and who made it

I built this site because I went to these three parks and did not want the trip to end up as a folder of photos I never opened again.

It started as a class assignment. We had to build three linked pages, and instead of picking a topic at random I used Sequoia, Zion and Bryce Canyon, because I had actually been to all three. That made the whole thing easier to write. I already knew what the trees felt like standing under them and how cold the morning was at Bryce, so the text came from memory rather than from copying facts off a website. Once I had a real reason to care how it looked, I ended up putting a lot more work into it than the assignment needed.

Angels Landing is the part that pushed me to actually finish it. That hike was the most scared I have been and also the best view I have ever climbed to, and both of those are hard to get across in a caption. Giving it a full page with room to write felt like the right way to keep it.

I am a computer science student at Georgia State, so the other reason I kept going was that I wanted the practice. Everything here is hand written HTML and CSS with no frameworks. The layout on this page uses CSS Grid, the other three pages use flexbox, and all the styling lives in one external stylesheet so I only write it once. Building something I actually wanted to look at taught me more than another practice exercise would have.
